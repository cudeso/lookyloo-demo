FAQ

Common questions.

Will monitoring slow down my website?

No. Each location sends one lightweight request per check, about eight requests a minute in total. That is less traffic than a single visitor browsing your shop.

Can you monitor pages behind a login?

Yes, on the Business and Operations plans. You create a dedicated test account for us and we store its credentials encrypted in our EU data centre.

Where is my data stored?

All check results and reports are stored in Frankfurt and Amsterdam. We do not transfer data outside the European Economic Area.

What happens if a probe location itself has problems?

An alert needs at least two locations to agree. If one probe fails, the others keep checking and our own operations team is paged instead of you.
