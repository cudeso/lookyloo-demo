Board rules. Read them. Staff will not repeat them.

  1. Use an alias. Never post under a name, email or handle you use anywhere else. Your opsec is your problem.
  2. Respect other members. Argue about deals, not about people. Insults and flooding get you muted.
  3. No doxxing. Do not post anyone's real name, address, photo or employer. Members, staff and outsiders alike. Instant ban.
  4. No real credentials in public threads. Samples must be masked. Posting live logins in the open burns them for everyone.
  5. Do not impersonate staff. Moderators have coloured names and never ask for payment in PM. Fake staff are banned on sight.
  6. Disputes go in Disputes. Scam calls anywhere else are deleted. Bring evidence: deal thread, timestamps, escrow ID.
  7. Vendors use escrow until they hold the verified badge. The badge can be removed at any time.
  8. One account per person. Rep farming with alts means losing all of them.
  9. English, Russian or German only in public sections. Keep translations short.
  10. Staff decisions are final. Appeals by PM to vex, once.
